# Product Vision

## ExamFlow ERP
**Simple Examination Management for Every Institution**

ExamFlow is a generalized examination-management and result-processing platform. The interface stays simple while academic rules, calculations, approvals, auditability, and historical integrity remain rigorous underneath.

## Principles
1. Simple for ordinary users.
2. Academic rules are configurable.
3. One authoritative calculation engine.
4. Official results are immutable after lock.
5. Consequential actions are audited.
6. Staging and Production are separate.
7. Free-tier-first during MVP validation.
8. Progressive disclosure.
9. No silent academic-rule fallbacks.
10. Every official result is traceable to the rules used.
